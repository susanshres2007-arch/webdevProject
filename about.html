About Birchline

Plywood furniture, designed in-house and made in small batches, with very little going to waste.

Our story

We started in 2019 in a shared garage with one CNC router and a stack of birch sheets. The first pieces were stools for friends. Then friends of friends asked for shelves, and the garage got crowded fast.

Today we design everything in-house and work with two local mills. Nothing gets thrown away: offcuts become shelf pins and coasters.
